# Product

## Register

product

## Platform

web

## Users

SaaS businesses and finance teams who need subscription billing management

## Product Purpose

Handle the complexity of subscription billing — proration, upgrades, downgrades, cancellations — without errors

## Brand Personality

financial, authoritative, trustworthy

## Anti-references

- Generic SaaS templates with purple-to-blue gradients
- Over-designed dashboards where data is buried in decoration
- AI-generated interfaces that all share one look (Inter font, cream backgrounds, rounded everything)
- Enterprise apps that confuse density with professionalism

## Design Principles

- Proration math that users can verify, not just trust
- Invoice states that never get stuck
- MRR dashboards that explain changes, not just show them
- Multi-tenant means isolated billing, shared billing engine

## Accessibility & Inclusion

- WCAG 2.1 Level AA compliance target
- Proper color contrast ratios (body text >= 4.5:1, large text >= 3:1)
- Keyboard navigable interface
- Screen reader friendly semantic HTML
- Support for prefers-reduced-motion and prefers-color-scheme
